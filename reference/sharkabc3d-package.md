# sharkabc3d: Shark and Ray Abiotic Covariates in 3 Dimensions

Facilitates the analysis of shark and ray habitat in 3D, enabling
descriptions of habitat by depth and area. Provides tools for volume
calculation, environmental extraction, and spatial overlap analysis
between species ranges, fisheries, and depth-stratified environmental
data.
